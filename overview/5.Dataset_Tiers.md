# 5. Dataset Tiers

## Question
How should a three-tier dataset offering map to NVIDIA tools, and what capture methods and buyer segments will shape the workflow?

## Recommended Tier Structure

| Tier | Deliverable | Suggested NVIDIA Stack |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: Curated real-world video** | Clean clips from specialized environments, with captions and physical-action labels | **Cosmos Curator + Cosmos Reason** |
| **Tier 2: Augmented video variations** | Real-video variants with changes to lighting, weather, glare, or textures | **Cosmos Transfer** |
| **Tier 3: Interactive simulation** | Digital twins, synthetic sensor data, physics simulation, and AI-policy testing | **Omniverse + Isaac Sim Replicator** |

## Key Guidance

- Use **Cosmos Transfer** for visual variations of real-world video. Use **Omniverse** only when a 3D reconstruction or interactive simulation is required.
- Reserve **Isaac Sim** for physics-based environments, synthetic modalities such as depth and segmentation, and closed-loop robot testing.
- Use owned or properly licensed video, annotations, 3D scans, and OpenUSD assets. Verify redistribution rights for every third-party asset.
- Confirm the current NVIDIA license terms, hardware restrictions, attribution requirements, and commercial-use conditions before launch. Do not assume that every component is royalty-free or that all generated outputs have identical terms.

## Inputs Needed

1. **Capture method:** 2D cameras, depth cameras, or 3D spatial scans.
2. **Target buyers:** robotics companies, industrial automation teams, or vision-language model developers.

Practical Monetization Strategies

• Land-and-Expand Funnel: Sell Tier 1 at an accessible entry price to help AI teams validate that your niche domain improves their perception models. Upsell Tier 2 when their models fail on lighting or weather edge cases, and pitch Tier 3 once they move into robotics simulation.
• Non-Exclusive vs. Exclusive Licensing: Keep Tiers 1 and 2 non-exclusive so you can sell the same base manufacturing or lab datasets to multiple AI companies. Charge a steep exclusivity premium (3x–5x) if a buyer wants private rights to a specific facility or Tier 3 simulation environment.
• Custom Scenario Retainers: For Tier 3, offer a base OpenUSD environment fee plus a usage or custom-scenario fee when buyers ask you to run specific robot models or failure edge cases inside Isaac Sim.
